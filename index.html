Saltwater

Ambient sleep sessions, published nightly.

Saltwater publishes long-form ambient audio for sleep — one to two hours at a time, no talking, no interruptions, no sudden changes in volume. A short clip goes up alongside each session.

Everything is published to the Saltwater channel on YouTube. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

About this site

This site exists to host the privacy policy and terms for Saltwater Pipeline, the private tool that renders and uploads those videos. It is a personal automation tool operated by the channel owner. It has no users other than its owner, and no public sign-in.
